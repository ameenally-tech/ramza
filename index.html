Add Ramza to your home screen

It opens in its own window like a normal app — no browser bars swallowing the word, no address bar to tap by mistake, and it keeps working without signal once the cards are loaded.

ON IPHONE

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add, then open Ramza from your home screen.

ON ANDROID

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Choose Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Open Ramza from your home screen.

Staying in the browser for now? Turn the phone sideways, tap the lines icon just left of the web address, and choose Hide Toolbar — the one with two arrows pointing apart. It comes back as soon as you scroll, which is why the home screen is better.

Opened this from WhatsApp? You'll need to switch to a real browser first — tap the ⋯ or share icon and choose Open in Safari or Open in Chrome, then follow the steps above.
